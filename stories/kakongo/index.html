Kakongo: The Kingdom on the Atlantic Coast

A small kingdom of Central Africa that became a major trading power.

African HistoryEmpires & Lost Worlds Republic of the Congo, Angola1600s-1800s Coming soon

Kakongo stood on the Atlantic coast of Central Africa, in today's Republic of the Congo and Cabinda, Angola. With its neighbours Loango and Ngoyo it became an important political and commercial centre from the 17th to the 19th century.
