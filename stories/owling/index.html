The Owlers: England's Night-Time Wool Smugglers

For 450 years, smuggling wool out of England was a crime. The smugglers worked at night.

True Crime United Kingdom, France1367-1824 Coming soon

Taking sheep or wool from England to another country – especially France – was illegal from 1367 until 1824. The smugglers were called owlers, and their boats owling boats.
