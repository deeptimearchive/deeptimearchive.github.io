1. About our content

Deep Time Archive publishes documentary-style videos about history, mysteries and true crime for education and entertainment. We research each story carefully and list our sources, but history is incomplete: new evidence can change what we know, and some details are uncertain or disputed. Where that is the case, we say so.

Our videos are not legal, medical or financial advice. Nothing in a true-crime story should be read as an accusation against anyone a court has not found responsible.

2. AI tools

We use AI tools to help draft scripts and to narrate our videos (the narrator is an AI voice). Visuals are real archival photographs, maps and graphics, credited in each video and its description. Any AI-generated visual is labelled as a reconstruction. Every video is reviewed and approved by a person before publication.

3. Copyright and credits

Our scripts, narration, music, maps and graphics are our own work. Archival images are used under their licences (public domain or Creative Commons), and the required credits appear in each video description. You may link to and share our videos using the platforms' share features. Please don't re-upload our videos or reuse our original material without permission.

4. Platforms

Our videos are hosted on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram. Watching them is subject to those platforms' own terms, including the YouTube Terms of Service. Our publishing tool uses YouTube API Services; see our Privacy Policy.

5. Corrections

If you believe something in our content is inaccurate, please tell us. We review every correction request and, where we were wrong, we correct the record and say so.
