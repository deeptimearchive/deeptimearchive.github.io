Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

This policy covers two things run by The Deep Time Archive ("we", "us"):

  • This website, and
  • Deep Time Archive Publisher, a private tool we use to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account.

In short: this website has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. If you send us a story, we use it only to consider it for a video. Our publishing tool is used only by us, on our own accounts. We don't sell, rent or share personal data.

1. This website

The website is a set of static pages. It has no sign-up, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and it loads no third-party scripts or fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep them secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The search box works inside your browser: what you type is not sent to us or anyone else.

Videos on story pages

Story pages show a picture of the video first. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play. When you do, the video plays from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), under the Google Privacy Policy.

Share buttons

The WhatsApp, X and Facebook buttons are plain links. Nothing is shared with those services unless you click one, and then their own privacy policies apply.

Story submissions and messages

If you use the Submit a story form, we receive what you type: your name, email address, country, the story and any source you add, and whether you'd like to be credited. The form is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, which processes it only to send us the email.

  • We use your submission only to consider the story, to reply to you, and – if you agreed – to credit you by name in a video or article based on it.
  • We never publish your email address and never add you to a mailing list.
  • We delete submissions we don't use within 12 months, and any submission at any time on request.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and we delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed.

2. Deep Time Archive Publisher and YouTube API Services

Deep Time Archive Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the account holder agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The tool is not offered to the public. Its only user is the Deep Time Archive channel owner, who signs in with their own Google account and authorises it for the Deep Time Archive channel.

What it accesses

  • From the authorised YouTube channel: the channel's ID and title, and the status of videos the tool uploaded (for example, whether a video is private or public).
  • It sends to YouTube: our own videos, their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and caption files.
  • It does not access viewers' data, comments, subscribers' personal information, analytics about individual people, or any other channel.

How it is used, stored and shared

  • Used only to upload and manage our own videos, and to check that each upload succeeded.
  • Stored only on the channel owner's own computer: the sign-in token (to stay authorised) and a publishing log of our own uploads (video ID, link, privacy status, date). Nothing is stored on any server we run.
  • Not shared with, sold to or disclosed to any third party, and not used for advertising.
  • No cookies or similar technologies are placed on anyone's device by the tool.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • The account holder can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google security settings page (Third-party apps & services).
  • When access is revoked, or on request to the contact below, we delete the stored sign-in token and any data obtained through YouTube API Services within 7 days. Stored YouTube API data that we keep for our own publishing log is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days.

3. Facebook and Instagram (Meta)

The same tool publishes our own videos to our own Facebook Page and Instagram professional account through Meta's official APIs. It reads only the Page's and the Instagram account's basic profile information (name, ID, username) needed to publish, and it does not access messages, comments, followers' personal data or ads. Tokens are stored only on the channel owner's computer and are never shared. Access can be removed at any time in Facebook settings under Business integrations; we then delete the stored token within 7 days. Meta's practices are described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

4. Watching our videos

When you watch, like, comment on or subscribe to our videos on YouTube, Facebook or Instagram, that activity is handled by those platforms under their own privacy policies. We only see the aggregate statistics the platforms show every channel owner.

5. Children

Our content is made for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

7. Contact
